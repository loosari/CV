Profile

As a psychologist with a Master's degree in General Psychology, I specialize in psychological resilience, emotional and cognitive regulation, and cancer-related mental health. My research focuses on how individuals, especially those with chronic illnesses like cancer, manage emotional and cognitive challenges. Through extensive peer-reviewed publications, I aim to bridge research and practice, developing evidence-based strategies to enhance mental health outcomes. Committed to advancing the field, I work to create innovative solutions that support resilience and well-being in vulnerable populations.
